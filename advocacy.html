The Bigger Picture: How Do We Change?

The Roadmap to Revitalisation

Moving past pure documentation toward functional, structural infrastructure solutions.

CURRICULUM RESEARCH →

1. Systemic Curricular De-Colonisation

Integrating mother tongues directly into primary and secondary curricula for technical and scientific study, entirely removing the institutional boundaries that relegate native language beneath foreign systems.

MEDIA PRESTIGE STUDY →

2. High-Visibility Digital Media Production

Leveraging modern digital broadcasting and content networks to run native-tongue media, expanding the public presence of the language and converting historical generational shame into active cultural prestige.

AI INFRASTRUCTURE PORTAL →

3. Open-Source Digital Empowerment

Building localised digital infrastructure through text collection, custom alphabet fonts, and keyboard sets to feed low-resource languages directly into AI, translation, and user-interface development loops.

But most importantly, we should all honour ourselves, and be proud of who we are. African borders were not made by Africans. A lot of our countries still have to face the issue of avoiding conflict when choosing a native tongue, it is hard. But we, as the younger generation, can make greater efforts to actively learn our mother tongues and embrace them. They are not "vernacular" by any means. They are our heritage and our pride.
