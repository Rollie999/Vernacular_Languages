Foundational Languages

The Death Of Native Tongues

Africa is home to over 2000 languages. A quick Google search and deep dive will easily reveal this. But how many of those languages are actually spoken? Ask any African Teen if they speak their native tongue. If they do, ask them if they can speak that tongue purely without using substitute words from another language. And if they still pass this test, ask them if they can speak as fluently as their grandparents. Now you will find that most urban kids fail this test. In fact, most teenagers out there fail this test. And the reason is not that they never wanted to learn their tongue. No, African languages are currently in danger, and we, as the African society, are quietly ensuring it fades out. From parental refusal to pass on the language, to the simple fact that we call our native tongues *vernacular*, or unimportant, not really needed for our current lives. And it may seem like our decisions to do so are not harsh, but in the bigger picture, what does this actually mean?
